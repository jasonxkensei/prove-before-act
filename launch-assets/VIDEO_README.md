# Product Hunt video package

## Direction

- English AI narration with French subtitles.
- Dark PBA visual system and current product UI.
- The first demo export is explicitly labelled as a controlled demonstration.
- No claim is made that a blockchain transaction was sent.

## Deliverables

- `pba-product-hunt-launch-bilingual.mp4`: short launch film.
- `pba-controlled-demo-bilingual.mp4`: guided product demonstration.
- `product-hunt-launch-fr.srt`: editable French launch subtitles.
- `controlled-demo-fr.srt`: editable French demo subtitles.

## Real-network replacement

When the blockchain service and signer are healthy again, replace the controlled
capture with a real transaction recording. Keep the same narration structure,
but remove the controlled-demo notice only after independently verifying the
transaction and its public proof.
